Today's Session

Trail Walk

Before You Start

Warm up 3–5 minutes. First lap easy. Talk-test pace. No backpack during Weeks 1–4.

Uphill

Shorten steps as grade increases. Slight lean from the ankles, not the waist. Maintain effort rather than speed.

Downhill

Short controlled steps, soft knees, look 2–4 steps ahead. Slow immediately if the right ankle feels uncertain.

Footwear

Merrell Moab 3 non-waterproof. Track hot spots, toe pressure, heel/arch pain, ankle stability, and wet-shoe behavior.

Mobility + Recovery

Calf Stretch

20–30 sec × 2/side.

Soleus / Ankle

20–30 sec × 2/side with back knee slightly bent.

Hamstring

20–30 sec × 2/side; hinge from hips.

Hip Flexor

20–30 sec × 2/side; gentle pelvic tuck.

Ankle Circles

5 slow circles each direction per foot.

Session Order

Warm up → ankle bands → hip activation → balance → strength/core → mobility.
Pain rules: 0–2/10 is generally acceptable if movement remains normal and symptoms return to baseline by the next morning. At 3/10 reduce volume/range/resistance. Stop for 4+/10, sharp pain, swelling, limp, or ankle giving-way.
